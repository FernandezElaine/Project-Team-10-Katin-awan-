What Happens After Submission?

Every feedback record follows a simple review process.

1

Submission

Your feedback is securely saved and marked as Pending.

2

Administrator Review

The barangay reviews the concern and may mark it as Under Review.

3

Resolution

The administrator may provide a response and mark the concern as Resolved.
